The Grid

Four organisations. Pick a side.

Each has its own philosophy and its own idea of what security means. You join one in Discord, and from then on your rank, reputation and contribution count for it.

Null Division

Leave no trace. Trust no one. Move clean.

Null Division does not announce itself. It operates out of the unindexed sectors and recruits operators who value disappearance over influence. Membership is invitation-only on paper and deniable in practice. If a Null operator shows up on a leaderboard, they made a mistake.

Philosophy
The cleanest signature is no signature at all.
On security
Offensive minimalism. Anonymity is the only armor that scales. Reduce surface, route around the rest.

Vanta Collective

The Grid is no one's property.

Vanta Collective is a loose, decentralised crew of operators who reject the corporate stack on principle. Recruitment is by signal, not credentials; pseudonymous by default. Vanta builds the open relays the megacorps would rather you didn't have.

Philosophy
Resilience is a swarm property. There is no centre.
On security
Collective defense. Every member is a node, every node is expendable. Trust the mesh, not the keys.

Aether Systems

Order through architecture.

Aether Systems is the old establishment - the consortium that wrote the standards everyone else now follows. Members are long-game players: reputation is built across decades, not patches. Aether's badge opens institutional doors and closes underground ones.

Philosophy
Well-defined process is the only durable defence.
On security
Governance. Standards, audits, signed change records. A boring incident is a successful one.

CyTek Industries

Engineer the next layer of the Grid.

CyTek Industries is the Grid's largest engineering conglomerate. They build the relays, the protocols and the tools every other operator depends on. Members are pragmatists - they ship more than they argue. CyTek's badge buys credibility everywhere on the Grid, and a long list of people who'd like to see it scratched.

Philosophy
Build first, refine later. Tools beat manifestos.
On security
Pragmatic defense. Assume compromise, harden the next layer, ship the patch.
