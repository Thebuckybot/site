      How the monthly cache works

      You claim it, it is never dropped on you
      A new cache appears once per calendar month while your subscription is running. It waits until you run premium and press claim.
      An unclaimed cache cannot be stolen
      It is not part of your balance and not part of your net worth, so it is out of reach of robberies by construction - there is nothing there to take.
      One cache per month
      Subscribing twice in the same month still gives one cache. Upgrading mid-month lifts the waiting cache to the higher tier.
