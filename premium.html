        What is a Security Boost, and who is it for?
        A boost is a one-time purchase for a server: you buy it, then give it to a server where you have Manage Server. While it runs, that server keeps its security logs for a year instead of two weeks, holds 30 detection rules instead of 10 and 25 recovery snapshots instead of 10. It changes nothing about your own account.
        When does the clock start, and can I move it?
        The clock starts when you assign the boost, not when you buy it, so a boost in reserve does not run out. After seven days you can move it to another server; the server it leaves keeps its limits for seven more days. A server's admins can decline a boost; it then goes back to your reserve with its clock still running.
        What happens when a boost ends?
        The server keeps its extended logs, rules and snapshots for seven days. Assign a new boost in that window and nothing changes; otherwise the limits fall back to the free ones and older data is removed on the next hourly sweep. The server and its members are told in the server's #b-boost channel.
